Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items

If an item is damaged, defective, or incorrect, you may return it immediately to the delivery agent. You will not need to pay any delivery charge for that immediate return.

3-Day Return & Exchange Window

Returns and exchanges are accepted within 3 days of delivery when the item is unused, unwashed, and returned in its original condition with all packaging and tags intact.

Return Delivery Fees

  • If the return or exchange is due to a Tanda error, such as a damaged or wrong item, Tanda covers the delivery fees.
  • For a change of mind or general size/color exchange, the customer covers the delivery charges.
